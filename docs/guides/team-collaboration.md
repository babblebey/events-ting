## Introduction

The Team Collaboration feature enables event organizers to share event management responsibilities with collaborators. Instead of managing everything alone, you can invite team members and grant them access to specific modules based on their role.

### Key Benefits

**For Organizers**:
- Distribute workload across team members
- Grant access to specific features only
- Maintain full control over permissions
- Track who has access to what

**For Collaborators**:
- Help manage events you're passionate about
- Clear understanding of your responsibilities
- Access only the tools you need
- See all your events in one place

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

- Active Events-Ting account
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- Email access to receive invitations

### Quick Start (30 seconds)

**As an Organizer**:
1. Go to your event's Settings page
2. Click "Team" in the sidebar
3. Click "Invite Collaborator"
4. Enter email and select modules
5. Click "Send Invitation"

**As a Collaborator**:
1. Check your email for the invitation
2. Click "Accept Invitation"
3. Sign in to Events-Ting (or create account)
4. Start managing the event!

## For Event Organizers

### Inviting Collaborators

#### Step-by-Step

1. **Navigate to Team Settings**
   - Open your event dashboard
   - Click **Settings** in the sidebar
   - Select **Team** tab

2. **Click "Invite Collaborator"**
   - A form will appear

3. **Fill in Details**
   - **Email**: Enter the collaborator's email address
   - **Module Permissions**: Check the boxes for features they should access

4. **Review and Send**
   - Double-check the email and permissions
   - Click **Send Invitation**
   - Invitation email sent immediately!

#### What Happens Next?

- ✉️ Collaborator receives an email with acceptance link
- ⏱️ Invitation expires in 7 days
- 📊 You can see pending invitations in the Team page
- 🔄 You can resend or cancel the invitation anytime

#### Module Permissions Explained

When inviting a collaborator, you choose which modules they can access:

| Module | What They Can Do |
|--------|------------------|
| **Overview** | View event statistics and dashboard summary |
| **Attendees** | View, add, edit, and manage attendee registrations |
| **Tickets** | Create and manage ticket types and pricing |
| **Schedule** | Build and edit the event schedule, manage sessions |
| **Speakers** | Add and manage speaker profiles |
| **CFP** | Review, accept, and reject Call for Papers submissions |
| **Communications** | Create and send email campaigns to attendees |

**Note**: The **Settings** module is owner-only and cannot be assigned.

#### Tips for Choosing Permissions

- **Start minimal**: Grant only the modules they need initially
- **Expand later**: You can always add more permissions
- **Be specific**: Don't give full access unless necessary
- **Trust your team**: But verify permissions match their role

### Managing Your Team

#### View Team Members

The Team page shows:
- **Active Members**: Currently collaborating
- **Pending Invitations**: Awaiting acceptance
- **Declined/Expired**: Recent invitation responses

Each member card displays:
- Name and email
- Role (Owner or Collaborator)
- Assigned modules
- When they were invited
- Last access time

#### Update Permissions

Need to change what a collaborator can do?

1. Find their card on the Team page
2. Click **Edit Permissions**
3. Check/uncheck modules
4. Click **Save Changes**

**Changes take effect immediately!** The collaborator will be notified by email.

#### Resend Invitations

If a collaborator didn't receive the email or it expired:

1. Go to **Pending Invitations** section
2. Find the invitation
3. Click **Resend**
4. New email sent with fresh 7-day expiry!

#### Cancel Invitations

Changed your mind before they accepted?

1. Go to **Pending Invitations** section
2. Find the invitation
3. Click **Cancel**
4. Invitation invalidated immediately

#### Remove Collaborators

Need to revoke someone's access?

1. Find their card on the Team page
2. Click **Remove Member**
3. Confirm the removal
4. Access revoked immediately

**⚠️ Important**: Removed collaborators:
- Lose access to ALL modules instantly
- Cannot access the event dashboard
- Receive a notification email
- Can be re-invited later if needed

### Owner Responsibilities

As the event owner, you have special privileges and responsibilities:

✅ **You Can**:
- Invite unlimited collaborators
- Modify any collaborator's permissions
- Remove any collaborator
- Transfer ownership (future feature)
- Access all modules (including Settings)

❌ **You Cannot**:
- Remove yourself (ownership transfer required)
- Invite yourself (you're already the owner!)
- Modify your own permissions (you have full access)

### Best Practices

1. **Document Roles**: Keep notes on who does what
2. **Regular Reviews**: Check permissions quarterly
3. **Remove Inactive Members**: Clean up access periodically
4. **Use Specific Permissions**: Don't grant "everything" unless necessary
5. **Communicate Changes**: Let team know when permissions change
6. **Onboard New Members**: Show them around before granting access

## For Collaborators

### Accepting Invitations

#### Step-by-Step

1. **Check Your Email**
   - Look for "Invitation to collaborate on [Event Name]"
   - Check spam folder if not in inbox

2. **Review the Invitation**
   - Email shows event name, organizer, and your permissions
   - Click **Accept Invitation** button

3. **Sign In or Sign Up**
   - Existing user: Sign in with your account
   - New user: Create a free Events-Ting account

4. **Confirmation**
   - You'll see a success message
   - Redirected to the event dashboard
   - Organizer receives acceptance notification

#### What If You Don't Want to Collaborate?

You can **Decline** the invitation:

1. Click the invitation link from email
2. Click **Decline Invitation** instead of Accept
3. Organizer is notified of your decision
4. No account required to decline

**Note**: Organizers can re-invite you later if circumstances change.

### Your Collaborator Dashboard

#### Viewing All Your Events

Navigate to **My Teams** from the main menu:

- See all events you collaborate on
- View your role and permissions per event
- Quick links to each event dashboard
- Filter by role (Owner vs Collaborator)

#### Understanding Your Access

On any event dashboard:

- **Sidebar Navigation**: Shows only modules you can access
- **Grayed Out Items**: Modules you don't have permission for
- **Tooltip on Hover**: Explains why access is restricted

### Working with Limited Permissions

#### What You Can Do

You have **full access** to your assigned modules:
- View all data in those modules
- Create new records (speakers, tickets, etc.)
- Edit existing records
- Delete records (where applicable)

#### What You Cannot Do

You **cannot** access:
- Modules not in your permission list
- Event Settings (owner-only)
- Team Management (owner-only)
- Financial/billing information (owner-only)

#### Requesting More Access

Need access to additional modules?

**Option 1: Ask the Organizer**
- Send them a message explaining why you need access
- They can update your permissions instantly

**Option 2: Check Team Page**
- You can view (but not edit) the team list
- See who the owner is
- Confirm your current permissions

### Permission Changes

#### If Your Permissions Increase

✅ **You'll receive an email** showing:
- Which modules were added
- Which modules were removed (if any)
- Your complete new permission list

✅ **Changes are immediate**:
- Refresh the page to see new sidebar items
- New modules are accessible right away

#### If Your Permissions Decrease

⚠️ **If you're actively using a revoked module**:
- Your next action will return an error
- You'll see a notification: "Your permissions changed"
- You'll be redirected to an allowed module
- **In-progress changes may be lost**

**Tip**: Save your work frequently when collaborating!

#### If Your Access Is Removed

🚫 **Complete access revocation**:
- You'll receive an email notification
- Next time you try to access the event: "Access Denied"
- You're redirected to a notification page
- You can still see the event in your My Teams list (marked as "Removed")

**Can you be re-added?** Yes! Organizers can send a new invitation anytime.

### Collaboration Etiquette

1. **Communicate**: Keep organizers informed of major changes
2. **Stay in Your Lane**: Don't request access you don't need
3. **Be Responsive**: Accept invitations promptly
4. **Ask Questions**: Clarify responsibilities upfront
5. **Respect Permissions**: Don't try to circumvent access controls
6. **Give Notice**: Let organizers know if you need to step away

## Understanding Permissions

### Permission Model

Events-Ting uses **module-level permissions**:
- Access is **binary**: You either have full access or no access
- No "view-only" or "edit-only" within modules
- Permissions are **explicit**: Must be granted per module

### Module Details

#### OVERVIEW
**What it is**: Event dashboard home page  
**What you can do**: View statistics, registration counts, revenue metrics  
**What you cannot do**: Modify any settings (read-only module)  
**Typical role**: Marketing team, volunteers

#### ATTENDEES
**What it is**: Registration and attendee management  
**What you can do**: View attendees, edit registrations, check-in guests, export lists  
**What you cannot do**: Modify ticket types (requires Tickets permission)  
**Typical role**: Registration coordinators, front desk volunteers

#### TICKETS
**What it is**: Ticket type and pricing management  
**What you can do**: Create ticket types, set prices, manage capacity, create discount codes  
**What you cannot do**: Process refunds (owner-only)  
**Typical role**: Operations team, pricing managers

#### SCHEDULE
**What it is**: Event schedule builder  
**What you can do**: Create sessions, assign speakers, set times, manage tracks  
**What you cannot do**: Add speakers (requires Speakers permission)  
**Typical role**: Program managers, schedule coordinators

#### SPEAKERS
**What it is**: Speaker profile management  
**What you can do**: Add speakers, edit bios, upload photos, manage contact info  
**What you cannot do**: Assign to sessions (requires Schedule permission)  
**Typical role**: Speaker liaison, program committee

#### CFP
**What it is**: Call for Papers submissions  
**What you can do**: View submissions, accept/reject proposals, leave review comments  
**What you cannot do**: Modify CFP questions (owner-only)  
**Typical role**: Program committee, review panel

#### COMMUNICATIONS
**What it is**: Email campaign management  
**What you can do**: Create emails, schedule campaigns, view analytics  
**What you cannot do**: Change sender domain (owner-only)  
**Typical role**: Marketing team, communications coordinators

### Permission Combinations

**Common Patterns**:

| Role | Typical Permissions |
|------|---------------------|
| **Program Manager** | Schedule + Speakers + CFP |
| **Marketing Lead** | Overview + Communications + Attendees |
| **Registration Team** | Attendees + Tickets |
| **Content Manager** | Schedule + Speakers |
| **Review Committee** | CFP only |
| **Volunteer Coordinator** | Attendees + Overview |

### Owner vs Collaborator

| Feature | Owner | Collaborator |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Access Settings | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Invite Others | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Modify Permissions | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Remove Members | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Transfer Ownership | ✅ Yes (future) | ❌ No |
| Access All Modules | ✅ Yes (always) | ⚠️ Only assigned |
| View Team List | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes (read-only) |
| Remove Self | ❌ No | ✅ Yes (by declining) |

## Common Workflows

### Workflow 1: Onboarding a CFP Reviewer

**Scenario**: You need help reviewing conference talk submissions.

1. **Organizer**: Invite reviewer with **CFP** permission only
   ```
   Email: reviewer@example.com
   Permissions: ☑ CFP
   ```

2. **Reviewer**: Accept invitation via email

3. **Reviewer**: Review submissions at their own pace
   - Access only CFP module
   - Cannot see attendees or schedule
   - Can accept/reject proposals

4. **After CFP Closes**: Update permissions to **Schedule + Speakers** so they can build the agenda

### Workflow 2: Rotating Registration Team

**Scenario**: Multiple volunteers help with check-in during the event.

1. **Before Event**: Invite volunteers with **Attendees** permission
   ```
   Email: volunteer1@example.com, volunteer2@example.com, etc.
   Permissions: ☑ Attendees
   ```

2. **During Event**: Volunteers check in attendees
   - View attendee list
   - Mark as checked-in
   - Cannot modify tickets or settings

3. **After Event**: Remove access
   - Click "Remove Member" for each volunteer
   - They receive thank-you notification
   - Data remains secure

### Workflow 3: Marketing Team Collaboration

**Scenario**: Marketing team needs to send updates and track registrations.

1. **Invite Marketing Lead**: Grant **Communications + Attendees + Overview**
   ```
   Email: marketing@example.com
   Permissions: ☑ Communications ☑ Attendees ☑ Overview
   ```

2. **Marketing Lead**: Create email campaigns
   - View registration metrics (Overview)
   - Segment attendees (Attendees)
   - Send targeted emails (Communications)

3. **Expand Permissions**: Later add **Schedule** for promoting sessions

### Workflow 4: Handling a Permission Change

**Scenario**: Collaborator needs temporary additional access.

1. **Collaborator**: Request access via email/Slack
   > "Hey! Can I get Schedule access to update session times?"

2. **Organizer**: Update permissions
   - Go to Team page
   - Find collaborator
   - Edit Permissions → Add Schedule
   - Save

3. **Collaborator**: Receives email notification
   - Refresh dashboard
   - Schedule module now visible
   - Complete the work

4. **Organizer**: Revoke temporary access when done
   - Edit Permissions → Remove Schedule
   - Save

## Troubleshooting

### "I didn't receive the invitation email"

**Possible causes**:
1. Email in spam/junk folder
2. Typo in email address
3. Email delivery delay (up to 5 minutes)

**Solutions**:
- Check spam folder
- Ask organizer to verify email address
- Ask organizer to resend invitation
- Wait 5-10 minutes and check again

### "The invitation link says it's expired"

**Cause**: Invitations expire after 7 days for security.

**Solution**:
1. Contact the event organizer
2. Ask them to resend the invitation
3. They'll generate a new link (takes 10 seconds)
4. New link valid for another 7 days

### "I can't access a module I need"

**Possible causes**:
1. Permission not granted
2. Recent permission change
3. Access was revoked

**Solutions**:
- Check your permissions: My Teams → Find event → View permissions
- Contact the event organizer and explain what you need
- If recently removed, ask for reinvitation

### "My changes aren't saving"

**Possible causes**:
1. Permissions changed while you were editing
2. Internet connection issue
3. Session expired

**Solutions**:
- Refresh the page and check if module is still accessible
- Check internet connection
- Log out and log back in
- Contact organizer to verify your permissions

### "I'm seeing 'Access Denied' errors"

**Cause**: Your access was removed or permissions changed.

**Immediate steps**:
1. Check your email for notification from the organizer
2. Verify in My Teams dashboard
3. Contact organizer if this was unexpected

### "I want to stop collaborating"

**Option 1: Decline Future Invitations**
- Decline new invitations when received

**Option 2: Ask Organizer to Remove You**
- Send them a message
- They can remove you instantly

**Option 3: Simply Stop Using It**
- No action required
- Your access remains but you don't have to use it
- Organizer can remove you later

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: How many collaborators can I invite?**  
A: Unlimited! There's no cap on team size.

**Q: Can collaborators invite other collaborators?**  
A: No. Only the event owner can invite people.

**Q: Can I be a collaborator on multiple events?**  
A: Yes! You can collaborate on as many events as you're invited to.

**Q: Do collaborators need to pay for Events-Ting?**  
A: No. Collaborators can use a free account. Only the event owner needs a paid plan (if the event exceeds free tier limits).

**Q: Can I see who else is on the team?**  
A: Yes! All team members (including collaborators) can view the team list, but only owners can modify it.

### Permission Questions

**Q: Can I request specific permissions?**  
A: Permissions are set by the organizer. Ask them directly to update your access.

**Q: What happens if I try to access a restricted module?**  
A: You'll see an "Access Denied" page explaining that you don't have permission for that module.

**Q: Can permissions be time-limited?**  
A: Not currently. Permissions are active until changed or revoked. (Time-limited access is a planned future feature.)

**Q: If I have CFP permission, can I also modify the CFP form questions?**  
A: No. CFP permission allows reviewing submissions, but modifying the form is owner-only (under Settings).

### Security Questions

**Q: How secure are invitation links?**  
A: Very secure. Each token has 256 bits of entropy, expires in 7 days, and can only be used once.

**Q: Can someone else use my invitation link?**  
A: Technically yes, which is why invitations should be sent to correct email addresses and acted on promptly. Once accepted, the link becomes invalid.

**Q: What if I forward an invitation email?**  
A: Whoever clicks the link first and accepts will become the collaborator. Don't share invitation emails.

**Q: Can removed collaborators re-access the event?**  
A: No. Removed status is permanent until they're re-invited with a new invitation.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Do I need a special browser?**  
A: Any modern browser works: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions).

**Q: Can I use this on mobile?**  
A: Yes! The interface is mobile-responsive. Some features are easier on desktop though.

**Q: Will my changes sync in real-time?**  
A: Most changes save immediately. If multiple people edit the same record, the last save wins.

**Q: What happens if two people edit the same thing?**  
A: The last person to save overwrites previous changes. Coordinate with your team to avoid conflicts.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Collaborator** | A team member invited to help manage an event with limited permissions |
| **Module** | A feature area of the event management system (e.g., CFP, Attendees, Schedule) |
| **Module Permission** | Access granted to a specific module, providing full CRUD operations within that module |
| **Owner** | The person who created the event; has full access to all features including Settings |
| **Invitation** | A secure, time-limited link sent to invite someone to collaborate on an event |
| **Team Member** | Any user with access to an event (owners and collaborators) |
| **Active Member** | A team member who has accepted their invitation and currently has access |
| **Pending Member** | Someone who has been invited but hasn't accepted yet |
| **Removed Member** | A former team member whose access has been revoked |
| **Token** | A unique, secure identifier used in invitation links |
